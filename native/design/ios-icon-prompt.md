# Connection iOS app icon

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The artwork combines two interlocking location pins in ivory and mint on the existing cobalt-blue brand background. The PNG is opaque with square corners; iOS applies the home-screen mask.

Production asset: `../ios/Connection/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png` (1024 × 1024). Xcode's asset compiler derives the device icon sizes.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS app icon artwork for Connection, a small private friend-and-family location sharing app. Generate a single beautiful, distinctive app icon, square 1024 x 1024. Full bleed opaque rich cobalt blue background with a very subtle royal-blue gradient, covering all four corners completely. Center a bold sculptural connected-location symbol: two softly rounded interlocking map-pin loops, a large ivory-white pin loop toward the upper-left and a smaller luminous mint-green pin loop toward the lower-right, their meeting suggesting togetherness and a fluid chain link. The negative spaces and downward pin tips should remain clean and understandable. Elegant soft 3D ceramic finish with restrained highlights and subtle contact shadows, crisp silhouette, modern premium friendly visual identity. Keep the actual symbol within the central 70% of the canvas with generous breathing room. Must be striking and readable at 60px on an iPhone home screen. One icon only, front facing. No text, no letters, no labels, no phone mockup, no frame, no rounded-square tile within the image, no corner rounding, no transparency, no watermark, no extra decoration. The entire output is the icon artwork itself, not a presentation.
